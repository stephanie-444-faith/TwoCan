How it works

Three steps. No sign-up sheet.

Two Can is built for people who want to help but don't want a committee. Show up with cans. That's the whole job.

1

Grab two cans

From your pantry or the store. Soup, beans, tuna, veggies, fruit. Two cans is the ask, more is welcome.

2

Gather at a meetup

A park, a library lawn, someone's driveway. Drop your cans in the bin, then stay for music, snacks, and people your age.

3

We give it away

Volunteers count the haul, post the total, and drive everything to the local food bank within the week.

What to bring

The cans food banks ask for most

Food banks can use almost anything sealed and in date, but some items go out the door faster than others.

Bring these

  • Canned protein: tuna, chicken, salmon
  • Beans and lentils, any kind
  • Hearty soups, stews, and chili
  • Vegetables, especially low sodium
  • Fruit packed in juice or water
  • Peanut butter and canned pasta

Pop-top lids are a bonus. Not everyone has a can opener.

Please skip

  • Anything in glass jars
  • Dented, bulging, or rusty cans
  • Past the "best by" date
  • Opened or homemade food
  • Anything that needs a fridge

Food banks have to toss these, so it's wasted effort for everyone.

About

Why a toucan?

Two Can was started by Sierra Kimmer with a simple idea: people want to help, and helping should be fun. A lot of young people have a couple of extra cans in the pantry and a free afternoon. Put those together with music and friends, and you get a food drive that feels like a hangout.

The name is the ask. Bring two cans. And "two can" do more than one, which is why every meetup is better with a friend along.

The toucan is our mascot because, well, it was right there. Also it's a bird with a giant beak that carries things, which is basically the job.
